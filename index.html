What to learn

Sync progress

Your statistics and hard pile are saved on this phone. To keep them safe across reinstalls and devices, they can be synced to the userdata branch of the app's GitHub repo (readable by anyone, since the repo is public). Paste a fine-grained GitHub token with Contents: read and write on vwurstep/Learn_the_country only.

Offline

The whole app (map, flags, summaries) is saved on the phone and works without internet, e.g. on a plane. Progress is saved on the phone and syncs when back online.
