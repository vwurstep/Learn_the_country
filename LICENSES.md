# Data and asset licenses

The files in `data/` and `flags/` are generated by `tools/build_data.mjs` (countries) and `tools/build_subdivisions.mjs` and `tools/build_sub_*.mjs` (subdivisions, see below) from the following sources.

- **Country shapes** (`data/world.geojson`): [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:50m Admin 0 – Countries, public domain, via [nvkelso/natural-earth-vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector) (GeoJSON), with Somaliland, Northern Cyprus and the Siachen Glacier dissolved into Somalia, Cyprus and India.
- **Flag positions** (`fx`/`fy` in `data/countries.json`): computed with a reimplementation of the pole-of-inaccessibility algorithm from [mapbox/polylabel](https://github.com/mapbox/polylabel), ISC License, Copyright (c) 2016 Mapbox.
- **Capital coordinates** (`data/countries.json`): Natural Earth 1:10m Populated Places, public domain, via [nvkelso/natural-earth-vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector); a few small territories were filled in by hand.
- **Country names, capitals, regions, UN membership** (`data/countries.json`): [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries), licensed under the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The data has been curated (one capital per entry, common English names, continent assignment).
- **Flags** (`flags/*.svg`): [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) by Panayiotis Lipiridis and contributors, MIT License (`flags/4x3/` set, v7.5.0).

## Subdivisions (`data/sub/`, `flags/sub/`)

Generated by `tools/build_subdivisions.mjs`.

- **Subdivision shapes** (`data/sub/us.geojson`, `data/sub/ch.geojson`): [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) Admin 1 – States, Provinces, public domain, via [nvkelso/natural-earth-vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector) (GeoJSON): 1:50m for the US states, 1:10m for the Swiss cantons. Coordinates are rounded (3 and 4 decimals), otherwise unchanged.
- **German and Austrian states, Italian and French regions, UK nations** (`data/sub/de.*`, `at.*`, `it.*`, `fr.*`, `gb.*`, also built by `tools/build_subdivisions.mjs`): shapes from the same Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 1 set (public domain); the Italian provinces, French départements and UK districts are dissolved into their regions / nations, and every set is simplified with [mapshaper](https://github.com/mbloch/mapshaper) (topology-preserving, 50% of the vertices kept, 3 decimals). Names: common English with the local name alongside (`local`, `capitalLocal`), curated in the script.
- **Canadian provinces, Australian states, South African provinces** (`data/sub/ca.*`, `data/sub/au.*`, `data/sub/za.*`, built by `tools/build_sub_ca_au_za.mjs`): shapes from the same Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 1 set (public domain), 3 decimals; Canada simplified with [mapshaper](https://github.com/mbloch/mapshaper) (topology-preserving, 25% of the vertices kept), Australia and South Africa unchanged; capital coordinates from Wikidata (CC0, as below; Victoria and Charlottetown picked by hand, Wikidata lacks the P36 statement); the Canadian and Australian flags are the Commons files listed below (all tagged public domain there; Prince Edward Island rendered to a 240 px PNG); South African provinces have no official flags (`flag: null`).
- **Chinese provinces** (`data/sub/cn.json`, `data/sub/cn.geojson`, built by `tools/build_sub_cn.mjs`): shapes from the same Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 1 set (public domain), simplified with [mapshaper](https://github.com/mbloch/mapshaper) (topology-preserving, 35% of the vertices kept, islands under 5 km² dropped, 3 decimals); capital coordinates from Wikidata (CC0, as below; the four municipalities use their own coordinate); English and simplified Chinese names curated in the script; no flags, as Chinese provinces have no official flags.
- **Capital coordinates** (`lat`/`lon` in `data/sub/*.json`): [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/), the subdivision's *capital* (P36) and its *coordinate location* (P625), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Subdivisions are matched by the Wikidata id Natural Earth carries or by their ISO 3166-2 code; a city-state (Berlin, Hamburg, Vienna) uses its own coordinate; where Wikidata lists two capitals the préfecture was taken (Dijon, Mamoudzou).
- **Flag positions** (`fx`/`fy`): the same polylabel reimplementation as above (`tools/geo.mjs`), ISC License, Copyright (c) 2016 Mapbox.
- **Names, capitals, colours**: curated in the build script; the colours are a fixed palette assigned by graph colouring.
- **Flags** (`flags/sub/*.svg`, `flags/sub/*.png`): the SVG files listed below from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/), used unchanged or, for the large seal-bearing US state flags, rendered to a 240 px PNG. All are in the public domain (US state flags are works of state governments or too simple to be copyrighted; the canton flags are centuries-old heraldry), except the flag of Mississippi (2020), which its designers released under Commons' "Copyrighted free use" terms (use for any purpose, without conditions). The German, UK and most Italian and French regional flags are public domain too; the Austrian state flags are the service flags with the coat of arms (`(state)` files, public domain), because the plain state colours repeat (Vienna, Salzburg and Vorarlberg are all red-white). The files marked **CC BY-SA 3.0 / 4.0** below (Apulia, Emilia-Romagna, Sardinia, Tuscany, Veneto, Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, Brittany, Occitanie, Pays de la Loire, Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur) are used under those licences ([3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), [4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)) with the attribution in the table; the PNG renderings of Tuscany and Veneto are shared under the same licence. Corsica is CC0. Subdivisions without an official flag have `flag: null` (Northern Ireland; Grand Est and Hauts-de-France, which Commons only has proposals for; Guadeloupe and Réunion, which fly the French tricolour). The *author* column is the uploader's credit on Commons (designer and/or vectoriser).
